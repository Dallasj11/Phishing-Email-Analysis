# Phishing Email Analysis

## Scenario

I analyzed a fictional email claiming that a student's school account would be locked within one hour unless they verified their password through a link.

## Warning signs I identified

- **Urgency:** The email claimed the account would be locked within one hour. This pressures the reader to act quickly.
- **Password request:** It directed the reader to a link to verify their password.
- **Unverified sender and link:** The email claimed to be from a school help desk, but I could not confirm that the sender or linked website belonged to the school.

## How I would verify it

I would not use the link in the email. I would open the school's website using an address I already trust and check for an account notice. If needed, I would contact the school's IT help desk using contact information from its official website.

## Assessment

**Likely phishing.** The urgent deadline and password request are strong warning signs. I would avoid the link and report the email through the school's normal phishing-report process.

## Limitations

This was a fictional practice email. I did not inspect a real sender's technical headers or visit the linked website. The `hxxps` spelling in the sample was used to prevent an accidental click; it was not itself a warning sign.
